Method

How the numbers are built

Blue-sky PT = (R3 × net margin × multiple × discount + excess cash) ÷ (shares × dilution). R3 is the revenue run-rate at the end of the 36-month horizon. Excess cash is zero for levered names, since their debt cost is already in net income. Targets are horizon values and are not discounted, except OKLO, whose 2032 fleet value is discounted three years at 15%.

Skew = (PT - price) ÷ (price - floor). Floors are cash or book per share (hard), or a trough multiple on FY1E consensus EPS or sales (soft). A soft floor assumes consensus earnings hold. Blue-sky is the outcome if the thesis fully works. It is not a probability-weighted value.
